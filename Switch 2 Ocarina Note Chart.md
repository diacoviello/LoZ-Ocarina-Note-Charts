# Every note on the ocarina, every way to play it

Five note buttons, each bent by the D-pad (half step) and the left stick (whole step). The two stack, so every button reaches three semitones either side of its natural pitch.

## Modifiers, in semitones

Tap Play to hear a reference tone. Easiest = fewest inputs, D-pad before stick.

Pitch names follow the original game's layout: L = D, R = F, A = A, Y = B, X = D an octave up. Nintendo hasn't published the remake's sounding octave, so treat the octave numbers as written pitch.

Bends affect free play only. Songs are recognized from the five natural notes.
