Pitch names follow the original game's layout: L = D, R = F, A = A, Y = B, X = D an octave up. Nintendo hasn't published the remake's sounding octave, so treat the octave numbers as written pitch.

Bends affect free play only. Songs are recognized from the five natural notes.
