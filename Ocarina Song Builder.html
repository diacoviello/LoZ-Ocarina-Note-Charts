Click a note to edit it. Click the grid past the last note, or the + button, to add one. With a note selected, click another row in its column to move it there. Drag the note editor by its title to move it, or by its bottom-right corner to resize it. Click a row name to hear that pitch. Solid notes are recorded from the game; striped notes are estimates.

b

Estimates speed up or slow down the nearest recorded natural note, which gets the pitch right but not each combination's own tone. Treat them as placeholders until they're recorded.

Easiest key tries every key that keeps the whole song between B3 and F5 and picks the one with the fewest modifiers, stick moves and claw grips. Moving the key or recommending fingerings replaces your combination choices; Undo brings them back.

Keyboard: ← → select notes, Esc closes the note editor, ↑ ↓ change pitch, + and − change length by a quarter beat, R makes a rest, D adds the same note after, Delete removes, Space plays. Notes are named at written pitch, like the other charts. Recordings show the game sounds one octave higher: R plays about 701 Hz, which is F5. Your song is saved in this browser only.
